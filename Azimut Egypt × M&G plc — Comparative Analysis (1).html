Case study highlights

Tap through each company's story.

M&G plc

From Prudential spin-off to digital-first savings brand

  • Demerged from Prudential in 2019 and re-branded around the M&G name.
  • Built M&G Wealth, combining advice, an online platform and a unified brand to serve mass-affluent savers.
  • Invested in simplified onboarding, app and online account access, and clearer reporting.
  • Pursued strategic partnerships and capital support to fund growth and technology.
Azimut Egypt

Bringing global asset management to a young market

  • Leverages Azimut Group's international product and research capability.
  • Targets a large, young, increasingly mobile population with little formal investing experience.
  • Operates in a market where instant payments, wallets and investing apps are opening the door to first-time investors.
  • Opportunity: be the trusted bridge between savers and capital markets.

How digital transformation changes the picture

Access, customer experience and financial inclusion — Egypt versus the UK.

Egypt

Mobile wallets, instant payments and investing apps are lowering minimum tickets and removing branch visits. Digital KYC is the key unlock, but trust and awareness still limit uptake.

UK

Platforms, robo-advice and open banking already make investing a few taps away. The challenge is moving savers from cash to investments and cutting the advice gap.

Egypt

Expectations are shaped by super-apps and wallets: instant, Arabic-first, mobile-first. Many investors still rely on phone calls and intermediaries for product explanations.

UK

Customers expect seamless onboarding, real-time valuations, transparent fees and clear outcome reporting, under the FCA's Consumer Duty.

Egypt

Inclusion is first about getting adults into the system at all. Fintech and wallets are driving this, and investment products can ride on the same rails as the next step up the ladder.

UK

Banking inclusion is almost universal. The gap is investment confidence, financial literacy and low-balance savers, so inclusion means making investing simple and affordable.

Lessons for Azimut Egypt from M&G

Practical takeaways, adapted to Egypt's context.

1

Build one trusted, simple brand

M&G unified its propositions under one recognisable name. Azimut Egypt can pair Azimut's global credibility with a clear local, Arabic-first identity.

2

Hybrid advice plus digital

M&G Wealth blends human advice with an online platform. Egyptian first-time investors need guidance too, so combine apps with advisers and call-centre support.

3

Lower the entry barrier

Low minimums, digital KYC, wallet and InstaPay funding, and recurring micro-investments can bring in new savers.

4

Make outcomes transparent

Borrow the UK's outcome-focused reporting: plain-language fees, goals tracking and clear risk labels to build trust.

5

Partner to scale

Strategic partners fuelled M&G's growth. Azimut Egypt can partner with banks, wallets and investing apps to reach users cheaply.

6

Invest in data and literacy

Use analytics for personalisation, and educational content to turn savers into investors.
